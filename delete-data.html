Your Rights

Delete Your Data

Cricket League App  ·  Data Deletion Request

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data at any time. Follow the steps below to delete your account and all associated data from the Cricket League App.

Option 1 — Delete from the App

1
Open the Cricket League App Make sure you are logged in to your account.
2
Go to your Profile Tap the profile icon or navigate to the Account section.
3
Open Settings Tap the Settings or gear icon within your profile.
4
Tap "Delete Account" Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account. Confirm when prompted.
⚠️ This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be deleted within 30 days.

Option 2 — Submit a Request by Email

If you are unable to access the app or prefer to submit a request manually, email us and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Send your deletion request to:

privacy@cricketleagueapp.com

Please include your full name and the email address associated with your account so we can locate and delete your data.

What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following personal data is permanently removed:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your account profile and settings
  • Any content or activity associated with your account

How Long Does It Take

Account deletion is processed within 30 days of your request. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been fully removed.

In some cases we may be required by law to retain certain information for a limited period. Any such data will be held securely and not used for any other purpose.
