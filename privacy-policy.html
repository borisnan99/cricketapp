Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

This privacy policy applies to the Cricket League App, a mobile application available on Android. The app is operated by an independent developer based in the United Kingdom.

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

Information We Collect

When you create an account or use the app, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Full name

We do not collect location data, payment information, or any other sensitive personal data.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your user account
  • Enabling you to participate in cricket league features within the app
  • Sending important notifications related to your account or league activity
  • Responding to support requests or enquiries

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices. We use Firebase (Google) for authentication and data storage, which complies with applicable data protection regulations.

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services which may process your data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, and push notifications
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method

We encourage you to review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR and applicable data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. To delete your account and data, follow the steps on our Delete Your Data page.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion of your account, we will remove your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

Children's Privacy

The Cricket League App is intended for general public use and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
